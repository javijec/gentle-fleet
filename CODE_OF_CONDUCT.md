# Community conduct

Contributors and maintainers are expected to treat others with respect, give constructive feedback, and welcome people of different backgrounds and experience. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer; use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for security vulnerabilities. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation to protect the community. Handle reports discreetly and fairly.
